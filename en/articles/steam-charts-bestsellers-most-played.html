Three Charts tabs, three meanings

  • Weekly top sellers (this article) — fixed 7-day revenue window with dates in the header.
  • Top sellers — broader bestseller view; timing may differ from the weekly slice.
  • Most Played — sorts by live concurrent players, updated continuously; not the same as weekly revenue.

Counter-Strike 2 can rank high on both weekly revenue and Most Played, but the metrics answer different questions: money vs. simultaneous players.

Notable rows for this week

Counter-Strike 2 leads with 723 weeks on chart — a long-tail revenue giant, not a one-week spike. MECCHA CHAMELEON (#2) is marked New. Hearts of Iron IV jumps ▲ 37 on an 80% discount (HK$ 63.60 vs HK$ 318.00).

Free-to-play rows dominate the top 50: PUBG, Wuthering Waves, Apex Legends, Where Winds Meet, Blue Archive, eFootball, Destiny 2, Limbus Company, StarSavior, Marvel Rivals, Once Human, Naraka: Bladepoint, and more — revenue comes from IAP/DLC, not the list price. Premium boxes include Forza Horizon 6, Monster Hunter Wilds, and Resident Evil Requiem (HK$ 498–518). Black Myth: Wukong and Once Human both climb ▲ 45 this week.

DLC and expansions appear as their own rows: Hearts of Iron IV: Thunder at our Gates (#23), DAVE THE DIVER – Jungle Content Pack (#43), and Destiny 2: Renegades (#45). Wallpaper Engine shows 258 weeks on chart (+10); utility software routinely sits beside AAA titles. Dead by Daylight has 476 weeks on chart at HK$ 38.00 after a 60% discount.

Column glossary (matches the store UI)

  • Price — HK$ on the captured storefront; discounts show base price and percentage; pre-orders and new releases are tagged.
  • Change — New, Re-entry, or week-over-week ▲ / ▼ positions; unchanged rows show —.
  • Weeks on chart — consecutive weeks in the weekly top-100 revenue list.

Weekly revenue vs Most Played

Most Played highlights who is running the client right now (Dota 2, CS2, battle royales). Weekly top sellers ranks store revenue in the printed date range. A deep-discount strategy title or new premium launch can outrank a game with higher concurrency but slower weekly spend. When reading news or forum posts, check which chart they cite.
