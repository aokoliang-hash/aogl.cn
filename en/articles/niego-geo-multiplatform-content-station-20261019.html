NIEGO-GEO content station — AI writing × multi-platform publish × GEO

NIEGO-GEO is a media-ops / GEO tool station I developed myself; the login brand line also reads NIEGO-AI. Product link: http://geo.niegoweb.com/—one workbench for enterprise multi-platform content management, AI drafting, and scheduled publishing. The login page states the pitch plainly: unified multi-platform content · AI creation · timed publish. This page is not third-party marketing copy. Positioning: own the content asset, don’t rewrite every app from scratch

Browser plugins that paste the same caption into five apps already exist. NIEGO-GEO’s bet is a durable enterprise workbench that keeps creation, materials, accounts, publish jobs, and performance feedback in one place. The sidebar roughly maps to:

  1. Workbench / AI assistant — turn natural-language briefs into library-ready content.
  2. Content library · material library — reusable masters and image assets, not siloed drafts.
  3. Publishing center · platform accounts — credentials plus scheduled distribution.
  4. Website articles · SEO / GEO optimization — owned long-form plus generative-engine visibility.
  5. Data reports · auto-publish rules · billing — closed-loop metrics and quota governance.
  6. Admin backend — tenants, members, audit logs, sensitive words, AI models, system params.

Psychologically, external platform traffic should serve an internal asset: a versioned master document. Blast-only tools lose rollback; CMS-only tools still force editors to hand-tune tone and character caps per network.

AI assistant: empty state answers “what can I do today?”

The assistant asks what AI should help with today—copy, images, titles, competitive analysis—with results saved into the content library. Six quick tiles cover high-frequency ops:

  • Xiaohongshu copy — spoken tone, auto hashtags.
  • Official-account long article — full structure, publish-ready.
  • Title generation — suspense, numbers, pain points.
  • Rewrite / polish — keep facts, raise shareability.
  • Generate illustrations — text-to-image into the material library.
  • Competitive analysis — playbooks and differentiation gaps.

The composer supports attachments and image gen; the model picker example is deepseek-chat. Session lists show pinned briefs like “autumn thermos Xiaohongshu copy,” which means conversations are treated as searchable creative assets, not disposable bubbles. Quota panels expose monthly publish and AI generation limits for free vs pro plans—honest SaaS cost boundaries belong in the article so readers do not assume infinite generation.

Main content editor: one master, shared by default

The edit view pins the master in the center: title, topic tags, cover, rich-text body. Sample copy notes that the body is shared across platforms by default. The right-hand AI panel toggles unified content vs generate by platform differences, with shortcuts for longform, Xiaohongshu tone, title optimization, and rewrite. A bottom “platform differences” strip opens WeChat Official Account, Weibo, and peers for overrides—or forces everything to follow the master.

Version history / rollback next to the master title matters for teams: AI drafts can overwrite careful human edits. When the cover slot is empty, editors pull from the material library or image-gen path; this aogl.cn article fills Open Graph with an auto-generated cover on the publishing side.

Per-platform generation: one brief, many tones and caps

In “generate by platform differences” mode, the brief box accepts natural language (for example three selling points for a September launch). Target platforms are multi-select tiles with length hints. A sample selection is Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and Instagram. Helper text states that per-platform generation calls the model once per platform and consumes points—cost disclosure instead of “one-click free forever.” Individual tabs can still follow or diverge from the master.

Semantically this is not template fill-in: Xiaohongshu wants spoken hashtags, Douyin wants short hooks, Instagram wants international tone. Pushing differences into model calls—not post-hoc editing—turns “multi-platform” into throughput. For GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), differentiated entities, citable structure, and owned long-form appear together; the sidebar places SEO and GEO optimization as sibling menus, signaling that visibility is no longer only classic blue-link ranking.

Website articles: owned long-form with cover slots

The “Website · temporary test site” tab shows main cover / video cover slots, title, topics, and structured rich text. Sample copy discusses choosing a content middle platform and the logic of a product launch, including the line that tools are leverage while methodology is the moat—aligned with NIEGO-GEO’s own stance. Unpublished state stays visible so editors do not confuse a draft with a live sync.

Platform channels: about twenty domestic and global exits

Admin “platform channels” cover major China networks (WeChat Official Account, Weibo, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Bilibili, Kuaishou, Zhihu, Channels, Toutiao, Baijiahao, Sohu, Ctrip, Meipian, and more) plus Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn. Cards show config status, global toggles, and parameter counts. Batch checks, token inspection, and “accounts expiring in 7 days” exist because the real multi-platform pain is often expired credentials, not prettier captions.

Data reports: publish is not the finish line

Reports summarize latest snapshots across published items: reads, likes, comments, shares. Detail rows include content, platform, account, publish time, engagement, and sync count, filterable by platform. Syncing stores snapshots so trends exist; a single live number is not a trend. Sample rows use a sandbox platform / demo account so the pipeline can be verified before spending real reach.

Architecture in one pass

  • Creation — AI assistant + master rich text + per-platform generation.
  • Assets — content library, materials, version rollback.
  • Distribution — publishing center, accounts, auto rules, ~20 channels.
  • Growth — website articles, SEO / GEO, analytics.
  • Governance — quotas plus admin (tenants / members / audit / sensitive words / models).

The split fits editor vs superuser roles. Unlike other first-party demos on this site (WordPet, EatToday, Prompt Share), this note sits in the content middle platform / multi-platform ops / GEO keyword cluster and works as a real SaaS product explanation sample.
