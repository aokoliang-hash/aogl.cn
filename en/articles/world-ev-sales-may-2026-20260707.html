BEVs rising, PHEVs in the red

Global plugin registrations reached ~1.7 million in May (+4% YoY). BEVs grew +15% while PHEVs fell -15% — the first five consecutive monthly declines for PHEVs since 2019. YTD plugins are only +2%, dragged by PHEVs (-11% YTD) while BEVs run +9% YTD.

In May, BEVs were 71% of plugin sales (~1.2M units). YTD split: 70% BEV / 30% PHEV, near the upper bound of BEV plugin share since 2014.

China & USA drag; rest of world surges

US incentive expiry (Oct 2025) and partial China incentive rollback (end 2025) weigh on the #1 and #3 EV markets. Excluding China and the USA, global EVs +39% YoY in May; BEVs +47%. May market share: BEVs 19%, all plugins 26%. 2026 YTD: BEVs 16%, PHEVs 6%, plugins 22%.

Best-selling models — May

No legacy OEM models in the Top 20. Tesla Model Y led with 93,571 (+16%). Geely Xingyuan / EX2 ~46,483 (+20%) in 2nd; Model 3 44,237 (+28%) in 3rd. BYD Song 4th despite -18% YoY; Yuan Up / Atto 2 5th; Leapmotor A10 8th in its third month.

Highlights: MG 4 record 20,054 (#12); Wuling Mini EV 18,330 (#13); AITO M6 ~18k in month 3; returns include Sealion 05, Mona M03, Wuling Bingo. Best legacy model outside Top 20: Toyota bZ4X (11,119).

YTD brands & OEM share

YTD models: Model Y sells ~2× the #2 Xingyuan. Five BYDs in the YTD top 10; Xiaomi places two models in the Top 20. YTD brands: Leapmotor 4th — past BMW and Volkswagen; Wuling 6th; Xiaomi 11th past Mercedes.

OEM plugin share (May): BYD 19%, Geely 10.1%, Tesla 8.1% ahead of VW Group 7.6%. BEV YTD ~5.2M units; BEV OEM: BYD 13.1%, Tesla 11.5%, Geely 8.9%.
