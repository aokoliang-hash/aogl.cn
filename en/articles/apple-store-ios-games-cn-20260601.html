#1: Honor of Kings S43

Honor of Kings remains the default CN iOS MOBA headliner. Store subtitle “S43 · Meet on the Path” marks a season transition window — subtitles often reveal the live ops theme better than rank alone. Spin-off Honor of Kings: World sits at #22 in this snapshot.

#2–#4: MMO launch + shooter stack

Lineage 2: Covenant (#2) is a classic-IP MMO launch pulse. Delta Force (#3) and VALORANT Mobile (CN) (#4) show tactical/hero shooters still dominate CN iOS discovery in H1 2026 — aligned with Today editorial around “瓦手” onboarding content.

#5–#10: Casual evergreen & party hits

Rich Little Sheep (#5) is a holiday-themed casual title. Game for Peace (#6), Happy Elements match-3 (#7), Eggy Party (#8), Love and Deepspace (#9), and Minecraft (#10) cover battle royale, puzzle, UGC party, romance, and sandbox — a six-genre top 10.
