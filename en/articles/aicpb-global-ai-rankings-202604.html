Website chart highlights

ChatGPT leads at 5.69B visits (-3.84% MoM). New Bing (#2, 3.58B) and Gemini (#3, 2.85B, +6.38%) follow. Claude (#4, 839.88M) posts the standout +34.18% MoM gain among top-tier chat products. DeepSeek, GitHub Copilot, and Grok (-14.43%) round out the AI-native block; Shop, notebooklm (+11.36%), and Nano AI Search (纳米AI搜索) show tool/commerce adjacency in the web top 10.

App chart highlights

ChatGPT leads MAU at 957.80M. Chinese super-apps dominate the rest: Doubao (豆包, 336.04M), Qwen (千问), Quark (夸克), Tencent Yuanbao, Baidu Netdisk, and Jimeng AI (即梦, +15.70% MoM). Google Gemini and DeepSeek bridge global and China-native adoption; Grok ranks #10 on App.
