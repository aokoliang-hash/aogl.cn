Electronics · Blink Plus plan at #1 · Apple & Fire TV dominate

#1: Blink Plus subscription

The Blink Plus Plan unlocks cloud clip storage and premium features for Blink cameras. Massive review count reflects installed base; holding #1 signals renewal velocity weighs heavily in Best Sellers. Do not confuse with Most Wished For or New Releases — Best Sellers tracks recent sales speed.

Apple stack: #2–4, 6, 8–9

AirTag 2nd Gen (#2) and USB-C EarPods (#3) are high-traffic accessories. Apple Watch Series 11 (#4) and AirPods Pro 3 (#6, ANC + live translation) anchor wearables and flagship TWS. AirPods Max 2 (#8, only 362 ratings but top-10 rank → launch surge) and AirPods 4 (#9, 31K ratings) round out audio. Apple holds six of ten slots — the clearest brand concentration signal on this chart.

Blink hardware: #5 & #7

Blink Video Doorbell (#5) and Blink Outdoor 4 (#7, Sync Module included) pair with the #1 subscription for a hardware + service loop. Add-On SKUs that require a separate Sync Module still rank when review volume and attach rates are strong.

Fire TV: #10

Fire TV Stick 4K Select (#10) with Alexa+ and AI search mirrors deeper-chart Echo Dot and Fire Stick variants (#11–14 in the full scrape). Streaming sticks remain a low-ASP volume lane — compare against Gold Box during Prime events.

Deeper chart (#11–38)

The full capture adds Echo Dot (194K reviews), multiple Fire TV Stick tiers, Beats Solo 4, iPad 11, Kindle lines, Insignia Fire TV 55", and Amazon Smart Plug (571K reviews). Third-party surge protectors, Roku remotes, and budget TWS fill the long tail.
