Reading Interbrand in 2026

Brand value ≠ market cap

Interbrand’s Brand Valuation™ (ISO 10668) combines financial performance, Role of Brand, and brand strength — not stock market capitalization. The hero infographic’s “16 companies over $1T” (CompaniesMarketCap, 16 Jun 2026) ranks NVIDIA ~$5.1T and Apple ~$4.4T by market cap; BGB 2025 puts Apple’s brand value at ~$471B and NVIDIA at ~$43.2B. Do not merge these rankings.

BGB 2025: AI and “Radical Realities”

The 2025 Top 100 totals $3.6 trillion, up $150B (+4.4%). Interbrand frames AI and agentic commerce as “Radical Realities”: delegated purchases vs brands humans still choose themselves.

Apple stays #1 for the 13th year (~$470.9B, -4%). Microsoft #2 (+10%), Amazon #3 (+7%), Google #4 (+9%). Instagram breaks into the Top 10 (#8, +27%). YouTube +61% to #13; Netflix +42% to #28.

NVIDIA rises +116% to $43.2B and jumps 21 places to #15 — the largest single-year brand value increase in BGB history. Twelve new entrants (most since 2000) include Booking.com, UNIQLO, BYD, and Shopify.

Best Japan Brands 2026: from Say to Act

Released 15 April 2026, BJB 2026 (18th edition) values Japan’s Top 100 at ~$318.9B (+1.5% YoY), slowing from +7.7% in 2025. Toyota leads for the 18th year ($74.2B, +2%). SoftBank posts the highest growth (+36%). Debuts: NTT, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, SEGA, SQUARE ENIX. Interbrand is expanding national/regional rankings in 2026 — BJB is a regional signal ahead of BGB 2026.
