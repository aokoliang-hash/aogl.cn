Reading Steam weekly top sellers

Three Charts entry points — do not merge them:

  • Weekly top sellers (this page) — 7-day revenue window with explicit dates.
  • Top sellers — near-real-time bestseller list; window may differ.
  • Most Played — concurrent players now, not weekly revenue.

Counter-Strike 2 can lead both weekly revenue and Most Played — money vs headcount are different questions.

#1 · Counter-Strike 2 (▲ 1 · 726 weeks)

CS2 stays #1 in US weekly revenue with an extreme 726 weeks on the top-100 chart. Free-to-play + cosmetics/pass revenue keeps it above most premium titles.

#2 · Marvel Rivals (▲ 3 · 83 weeks)

Marvel Rivals holds #2 F2P slot, ▲ 3. Hero shooter + Marvel IP competes directly with Overwatch 2 and Apex in US F2P revenue.

#3 · MECCHA CHAMELEON (▼ 2 · $5.99)

Low-price indie at $5.99 still #3 but ▼ 2 after last week’s surge — typical small-title launch spike pattern.

Biggest pulse: AC Black Flag Remaster marked New, ▲ 71 into top 10 at $59.99 — classic IP rerelease day-one revenue concentration.

#9 · ARK: Survival Ascended (▲ 55 · -75%)

ARK: Survival Ascended at -75% ($44.99 → $11.24), ▲ 55 to #9. Deep discount + survival genre = summer sale revenue spike.

#10 · EA SPORTS College Football 27 (Re-entry · New)

Field glossary

  • Price — US USD at capture; F2P shown as Free to Play.
  • Change — New, Re-entry, or ▲/▼ vs prior week; — = flat.
  • Weeks on chart — consecutive weeks in the top-100 revenue list.
