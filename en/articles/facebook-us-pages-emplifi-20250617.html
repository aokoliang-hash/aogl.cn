Most popular US Facebook pages (Jun 17, 2025): Emplifi likes/comments/shares archive

Netflix sweeps engagement · streaming dominates likes · Smith & Wesson top net fan growth

2025-06-17 · aogl.cn original

This page archives Emplifi’s 10 Most Popular US Facebook Pages 2026 blog (June 17, 2025). Rankings cover US brand pages by Like reactions, comments, shares, and follower growth for roughly Jun 2024–Jun 2025. Structured data: data/emplifi-facebook-us-pages-snapshot.json.

Facebook has 3B+ MAU. Emplifi ranks engagement within its US brand database — not Facebook’s official total fan leaderboard. Netflix’s ~197M page likes (Social Blade) differ from its 20.23M Like reactions in this chart.

Budweiser (575K comments) wins on cultural moments — Super Bowl, holidays, Clydesdales — prompting emotional discussion beyond typical CPG pages.
