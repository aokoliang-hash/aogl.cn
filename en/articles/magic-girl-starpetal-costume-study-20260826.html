Why a separate magic-girl folder

This site already ships girl2 · Lyra’s butterfly Chrono-Lens line, home-girl soft knit interiors, and Elena’s sea-wind ritual sheet. Dropping a pink-beret sealing-staff kit into those folders would tangle wardrobe meaning: Lyra’s identity is lavender butterflies; home-girl is beige knit pearl braid; Elena is island priestess ritual UI. magic-girl isolates the classic mahou-shōjo costume language—beret, star staff, companion creature, card prop, soft wing FX—so QA checks stay comparable across stills and the motion cut.

For SEO I deliberately repeat entities in prose: Mira Starpetal, magical girl, mahou shoujo, pink beret, sealing wand, companion mascot, Clow-style card prop, cherry blossom petals, white gloves, red bow. Crawlers meet HTML first; pretty pixels alone rarely rank long-tail costume queries.

Identity anchors I refuse to renegotiate

Like Lyra’s butterfly bible, Mira’s keep-set fails frames that break these checks:

  • Hair — short layered brown bob; one or two ahoge strands; bangs that still read in three-quarter light.
  • Beret + cherries — pink or white beret with cherry/bow accents; large red chest bow as the costume “logo.”
  • Staff — sealing / star wand with gold bird-or-crystal head; shaft must stay readable in action crops.
  • Companion — small winged yellow mascot when present; never swapped for a random animal mid-batch.
  • Palette — coral/pink, white lace, gold metal, sky blue; sakura pink as particle accent only.

Those rules turn “cute CGI” into a checkable character line. If a new still widens the jaw, drops the beret, or invents a neon cyber staff, it stays out of the keep set even if the lighting is prettier.

Costume ladder: battle dress, sailor badge, coral wings

The stills are not random outfits; they are a ladder of variants that must keep the same face:

  1. Action battle kit (pic.png) — layered white/red skirt, navy jacket accents, knee/elbow pads, inline skates, backpack, staff. Stress-tests silhouette under motion crop and petal bokeh.
  2. Pink battle dress bust (qjbpdj…) — puffy sleeves, lace trim, red bow, star wand, soft wing FX on warm yellow. Face ruler for green-eye / brown-hair continuity.
  3. Sailor-collar hopeful bust (srxi3i…) — white beret, wing-badge on shoulder, fingerless gold-plate gloves, clasped hands against blue sky. Checks whether identity survives a more “uniform” read.
  4. Coral dress + angel wings (2hgn58…) — larger feathered wings, iridescent feather FX on the fingertip. Escalates magic without inventing a new heroine.

Back view: bow, collar, wing overlay

zicj4d… is the reverse-angle QA frame. Magical-girl kits often look finished from the front and unfinished from behind. This still locks the red satin rear bow, white ruff collar, small feathered wing pack, and a translucent iridescent wing overlay on a pink/mustard split backdrop. If a later render invents a long braid or drops the bow entirely, it fails the back-view check even when the face is perfect.
