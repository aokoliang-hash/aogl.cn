Handy web tools (quick links)

Updated 2026-05-11

These are outbound links to established services. Privacy, medical accuracy, and terms of use are defined by each provider — not by this page.

This is a short curated rail for weather, translation, calendars, timers, quotes, QR, and converters — not a mega-directory of every SaaS on the web. Nothing executes on aogl.cn besides links out.

Last reviewed: 2026-05-19. I skip browser extensions that require opaque permissions and one-off scam “converter” domains.

How I pick tools: each tile must solve a task I hit at least monthly (check weather, translate a paragraph, schedule, time a deadline, glance at markets, make a QR, convert a file). I prefer the vendor’s own web app over random “universal toolbox” domains. Before pasting work documents into a converter, read that site’s retention policy — this page is only a bookmark, not an endorsement of unlimited uploads.
