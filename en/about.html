About this site

Last updated:

aogl.cn is a personal, non-commercial bookmark site for generative AI tools, official lab blogs, and a few vertical rails (games, shopping, social apps, and similar). One maintainer edits it in spare time; there is no company, sales team, or paid review program behind the lists.

Most pages combine hand-written notes (how a row is ordered, what is excluded, when it was last checked) with outbound links and, on some hubs, RSS headlines merged by scripts. Longer essays and demos live under articles/. This is not a newsroom: pricing, regions, medical use, and investment decisions always belong on each vendor’s own site.

What we do not do: sell products, guarantee rankings, scrape paywalled content, or present the site as financial, legal, or medical advice. Tool tiles and “top ten” rows are editorial shorthand aligned with public charts — not live counters.

Advertising: the site may show Google AdSense or similar ads. Ad networks are independent third parties; see Privacy for cookies and data practices. Ad placement does not determine which links appear in editorial lists.

Contact / corrections: see Contact for email and social links, or open a GitHub Issue with the page URL and what should change. Human-readable edits are also listed on Changelog.
