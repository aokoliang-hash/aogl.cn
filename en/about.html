About this site

Last updated:

aogl.cn is a personal author site. The primary content is long-form editorial demos and production notes (WebGL, video composites, character sheets, and first-party apps I built). Start at articles/ or the home carousel—not at Hub rails or tool tiles. One maintainer; no company, no paid placements.

Representative originals (review sample): WordPet · EatToday · Worlds Greatest (mnm) · img2threejs · site build notes · Lyra study. Sibling apps: wordpet.aogl.cn, eattoday.aogl.cn, mnm.aogl.cn.

AI tool tiles, Hub verticals, briefs, and tool guides are secondary bookmarks — mostly noindex,follow so crawlers and AdSense focus on articles. Chart/buy-guide archives stay out of the primary index. This is not a newsroom or link directory: pricing, regions, medical use, and investment decisions always belong on each vendor’s own site.

What we do not do: sell products, guarantee rankings, scrape paywalled content, or present the site as financial, legal, or medical advice. Tool name lists on the homepage are display-only (no outbound clicks).

Advertising: Google AdSense may appear on indexable content pages only. See Privacy. Ads do not decide editorial order.

Contact / corrections: Contact or GitHub Issue · Changelog.
