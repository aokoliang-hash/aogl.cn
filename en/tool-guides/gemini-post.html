Gemini

Chat & LLM

Note: Bookmark-style intro on aogl.cn — not the vendor’s official docs. Features and pricing are on their site.

Gemini is my go-to Google chat surface for fast image look-dev and brainstorming copy variants before they land in data/articles/. It sits beside Claude in the tools directory because the products overlap on text chat but diverge on multimodal defaults and Google account integration.

How I use it on this site

  • Hero stills — exploratory PNGs under original/role-girl/, original/character/, and similar folders (watermarks kept as provenance, not removed here).
  • Palette checks — quick “does this read as the same outfit?” questions before I commit a contact sheet.
  • Headline variants — three title options for changelog entries; I pick one and edit manually.

Workflow I actually follow

  1. Start from gemini.google.com linked below—signed into the Google account I am willing to use for experiments.
  2. State constraints: aspect ratio, wardrobe anchors, and “no logo” when generating art.
  3. Download to original/<project>/ with dated filenames; never overwrite canonical demo assets.
  4. Write an honest article paragraph about what shipped vs what stayed in the look-dev pile.
  5. For API or Vertex details, leave this page and read Google’s developer docs.

Gemini vs Claude (personal split)

Claude gets long English rewrites and careful code explanations. Gemini gets rapid visuals and Google-ecosystem questions. Neither replaces the other in my bookmarks—I list both so I do not pretend one tab solves every task.
