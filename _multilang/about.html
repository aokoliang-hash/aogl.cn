aogl.cn is a personal site whose primary content is editorial demos and production notes (WebGL, video composites, character sheets, and similar experiments). Start at articles/ or the home carousel. One maintainer edits it in spare time; there is no company or paid review program.

AI tool tiles, Hub verticals, briefs, and tool guides are secondary bookmarks — marked noindex,follow as of June 2026 so crawlers and AdSense review focus on articles. Most bookmark pages still carry hand-written notes (ordering, exclusions, last-checked). This is not a newsroom: pricing, regions, medical use, and investment decisions always belong on each vendor’s own site.

What we do not do: sell products, guarantee rankings, scrape paywalled content, or present the site as financial, legal, or medical advice. Tool tiles and “top ten” rows are editorial shorthand aligned with public charts — not live counters.

Advertising: the site may show Google AdSense or similar ads on content-primary pages. Ad networks are independent third parties; see Privacy for cookies and data practices. Ad placement does not determine which links appear in editorial lists.

Contact / corrections: see Contact for email and social links, or open a GitHub Issue with the page URL and what should change. Human-readable edits are also listed on Changelog.
