magic-girl · Mira Starpetal — mahou shoujo costume and sealing-wand study

original/magic-girl · beret · sealing wand · sakura action · video.mp4

2026-08-26 · aogl.cn original

This is a personal magical-girl costume and prop archive on aogl.cn. Every file lives under original/magic-girl/ (stills from late July–early August 2026; motion clip re-exported 2026-08-26). I treat the folder as a batch label for one silhouette family: short brown bob, pink beret, red bow, white gloves, sealing-style staff, and optional companion / card / wing accents. Working name on my private sheet: Mira Starpetal—not a client delivery, not an official franchise kit. The page walks real filenames so humans and crawlers can index phrases like “magical girl costume turnaround,” “sealing wand prop render,” “mahou shoujo cherry blossom action still,” and “personal CGI character consistency notes.”

Why a separate magic-girl folder

This site already ships girl2 · Lyra’s butterfly Chrono-Lens line, home-girl soft knit interiors, and Elena’s sea-wind ritual sheet. Dropping a pink-beret sealing-staff kit into those folders would tangle wardrobe meaning: Lyra’s identity is lavender butterflies; home-girl is beige knit pearl braid; Elena is island priestess ritual UI. magic-girl isolates the classic mahou-shōjo costume language—beret, star staff, companion creature, card prop, soft wing FX—so QA checks stay comparable across stills and the motion cut.

For SEO I deliberately repeat entities in prose: Mira Starpetal, magical girl, mahou shoujo, pink beret, sealing wand, companion mascot, Clow-style card prop, cherry blossom petals, white gloves, red bow. Crawlers meet HTML first; pretty pixels alone rarely rank long-tail costume queries.

I keep the Gemini filename prefixes for batch traceability—same habit as girl2 and sports-lens. I do not paste full vendor prompts or teach watermark removal. Genre silhouettes borrow from classic cardcaptor-era costume grammar; this archive is personal generative art and production notes, not licensed merchandise.

Identity anchors I refuse to renegotiate

Like Lyra’s butterfly bible, Mira’s keep-set fails frames that break these checks:

  • Hair — short layered brown bob; one or two ahoge strands; bangs that still read in three-quarter light.
  • Beret + cherries — pink or white beret with cherry/bow accents; large red chest bow as the costume “logo.”
  • Staff — sealing / star wand with gold bird-or-crystal head; shaft must stay readable in action crops.
  • Companion — small winged yellow mascot when present; never swapped for a random animal mid-batch.
  • Palette — coral/pink, white lace, gold metal, sky blue; sakura pink as particle accent only.

Those rules turn “cute CGI” into a checkable character line. If a new still widens the jaw, drops the beret, or invents a neon cyber staff, it stays out of the keep set even if the lighting is prettier.

Costume ladder: battle dress, sailor badge, coral wings

The stills are not random outfits; they are a ladder of variants that must keep the same face:

  1. Action battle kit (pic.png) — layered white/red skirt, navy jacket accents, knee/elbow pads, inline skates, backpack, staff. Stress-tests silhouette under motion crop and petal bokeh.
  2. Pink battle dress bust (qjbpdj…) — puffy sleeves, lace trim, red bow, star wand, soft wing FX on warm yellow. Face ruler for green-eye / brown-hair continuity.
  3. Sailor-collar hopeful bust (srxi3i…) — white beret, wing-badge on shoulder, fingerless gold-plate gloves, clasped hands against blue sky. Checks whether identity survives a more “uniform” read.
  4. Coral dress + angel wings (2hgn58…) — larger feathered wings, iridescent feather FX on the fingertip. Escalates magic without inventing a new heroine.

Prop studies: wand macro and seal card

Props get their own stills so the heroine does not have to carry every QA burden:

  • beqzsh… — diagonal macro of a pink-crystal crown wand head on apricot bokeh. Specular gold vs translucent pink must match the handheld staff in portraits.
  • ukjdd8… — bordered card with gold sun–moon–wing seal geometry; index finger pointing at the center. Useful for “collector prop” crops and for locking red-border / gold-corner language.

Back view: bow, collar, wing overlay

zicj4d… is the reverse-angle QA frame. Magical-girl kits often look finished from the front and unfinished from behind. This still locks the red satin rear bow, white ruff collar, small feathered wing pack, and a translucent iridescent wing overlay on a pink/mustard split backdrop. If a later render invents a long braid or drops the bow entirely, it fails the back-view check even when the face is perfect.

Motion: video.mp4 + music.mp3

Stills hide flicker; a short motion pass exposes petal boiling, wing shimmer, and wand specular crawl. video.mp4 is about 44.5 seconds (~18 MB), archived 2026-08-26. music.mp3 sits beside it as the preview BGM bed so local review does not depend on a streaming license. I keep audio and picture as separate files—same pattern as other demos on this site—so the article page can embed video while still documenting the soundtrack path.

Workflow notes next to Lyra and Elena

Order that saved iterations: lock face + beret + bow anchors → prop macros (wand / card) → costume ladder stills → back view → only then cut video.mp4. When motion looked wrong, I fixed the still that owned the failing rule instead of painting inside the timeline. Hero generation for Open Graph still goes through npm run generate-article-covers from pic.png so the homepage carousel gets a 1200×675 WebP without inventing stock photography.

Chinese body copy serves regional queries around「魔法少女」「封印杖」「樱花特效」; English body copy serves “magical girl costume study,” “sealing wand prop,” and “CGI mahou shoujo consistency.” Short locales stay brief—the thick bilingual pair carries AdSense review weight. This line stays indexable primary content, not a ranking-table archive.

Accessibility and SEO checklist

  • Alt text names visible costume entities (beret, bow, wand, companion, card, wings)—never “image 1.”
  • Body text repeats Mira Starpetal, magic-girl, mahou shoujo, sealing wand, cherry blossoms, original/magic-girl/, video.mp4.
  • Video uses controls playsinline with a still poster; narrative remains in first-paint HTML.
  • No claim of official franchise partnership; personal generative-art archive only.

Boundaries

These files document a personal magical-girl aesthetic study I maintain under original/magic-girl/. Silhouettes echo classic mahou-shōjo costume grammar for learning consistency—not commercial cosplay commissions, and not medical, financial, or legal advice. Children viewing transformation tropes should have age-appropriate context from a guardian.

magic-girl · Mira 星瓣：魔法少女服装与封印杖道具手记

original/magic-girl · 贝雷·封印杖·樱花动势·video.mp4

2026-08-26 · 本站原创

这是 aogl.cn 上的一篇个人魔法少女造型与道具归档手记。全部文件在 original/magic-girl/（静帧约 2026-07 末至 08 初；动态片 2026-08-26 再导出）。文件夹是一条轮廓家族的批次标签：短棕发、粉色贝雷、大红蝴蝶结、白手套、封印杖气质的法杖，以及可选的伙伴 / 卡牌 / 羽翼特效。私人设定表里的工作名：Mira Starpetal（米拉·星瓣）——不是客户交付，也不是官方周边包。本文按真实文件名拆解，方便检索「魔法少女服装一致性」「封印杖道具渲染」「樱花动势静帧」「CGI mahou shoujo 手记」等长尾词。

为什么单独开 magic-girl 文件夹

本站已有 girl2 · Lyra 蝶纹时间透镜、home-girl 居家针织、艾琳娜海风设定表。把粉贝雷 + 封印杖塞进这些目录会搅乱衣橱语义：Lyra 是紫发蝴蝶，home-girl 是米色侧辫珍珠，Elena 是海岛祭司仪式 UI。magic-girl 专门承载经典魔法少女服装语法——贝雷、星杖、伙伴生物、卡牌道具、柔光羽翼——让静帧与动态片可以互相核对。

SEO 上会刻意复述实体：Mira Starpetal、魔法少女、mahou shoujo、粉贝雷、封印杖、伙伴吉祥物、卡牌道具、樱花瓣、白手套、红蝴蝶结。爬虫先读 HTML；只靠美图很难排到长尾服装词。

文件夹里有什么

  • pic.png — 蓝天下全身腾跃；樱花；伙伴；法杖；轮滑与护具。
  • Gemini_Generated_Image_qjbpdjqjbpdjqjbp.png — 粉色战斗裙半身、星杖、柔光翼。

保留 Gemini 文件名前缀便于批次追溯，习惯与 girl2、sports-lens 一致。不贴完整厂商提示词，也不教去水印。轮廓借鉴经典「卡片收集系」魔法少女服装语法；本稿是个人生成式美术与制作备忘，不是授权周边。

不愿重新谈判的身份锚点

  • 发型 — 短层棕发；一到两缕呆毛；侧光下刘海仍可读。
  • 贝雷 + 蝴蝶结 — 粉/白贝雷配樱桃或结饰；胸前大红结当服装「标志」。
  • 法杖 — 星头 / 晶冠封印杖；动势裁切里杖身仍要可辨。
  • 伙伴 — 出现时为小型带翼黄色吉祥物；批次内不随便换成别的动物。
  • 色板 — 珊瑚粉、白蕾丝、金色金属、天蓝；樱花粉只作粒子点缀。

这些规则把「好看的 CGI」变成可核对的角色线。新静帧若下巴变宽、丢了贝雷、或长出霓虹赛博杖，哪怕打光更美也不进保留集。

服装阶梯：战斗裙、水手徽章、珊瑚翼

  1. 动势战斗套（pic.png）— 白红层裙、护膝护肘、轮滑、背包、法杖。考验动势裁切与樱花景深下的剪影。
  2. 粉色战斗裙半身（qjbpdj…）— 泡袖蕾丝、红结、星杖、暖黄底柔翼。绿眼棕发的脸尺。
  3. 水手领祈愿半身（srxi3i…）— 白贝雷、肩章羽翼徽、指尖金属板手套、合十对蓝天。看「制服感」是否仍是同一人。
  4. 珊瑚裙 + 天使翼（2hgn58…）— 更大羽翼与指尖虹彩羽毛。抬高魔法感但不换脸。

道具研究：杖头微距与纹章卡

  • beqzsh… — 粉晶金冠杖头斜构图；金色高光与粉透折射要对齐半身图里的手持杖。
  • ukjdd8… — 红边金角卡牌，中央日月翼纹；食指点中心。用于「收集道具」裁切与边框语言锁定。

背视：蝴蝶结、领口、光翼叠层

zicj4d… 是反角质检。魔法少女套装常「正面做完、背面没做」。此帧锁定脑后红缎结、白色立领褶、小型羽翼包，以及粉黄分色背景上的半透明虹彩光翼。若后续渲染长出长辫或丢掉后结，即使脸完美也判失败。

动态：video.mp4 + music.mp3

静帧藏不住闪烁；短动势暴露樱花沸腾、羽翼闪烁与杖头高光爬行。video.mp4 约 44.5 秒（约 18 MB），2026-08-26 归档。music.mp3 作预览 BGM，本地审片不依赖流媒体授权。音画分文件存放——与站内其它 Demo 一致——文章可嵌视频并单独写明音轨路径。

与 Lyra / Elena 并排的工作流备忘

省迭代的顺序：锁脸 + 贝雷 + 蝴蝶结 → 道具微距（杖 / 卡）→ 服装阶梯静帧 → 背视 → 再切 video.mp4。动势不对时，先修「拥有那条失败规则」的静帧，而不是在时间线上硬画。封面仍从 pic.png 走 npm run generate-article-covers，给首页轮播 1200×675 WebP。

中文服务「魔法少女」「封印杖」「樱花特效」等地域意图；英文服务 costume study / sealing wand / CGI consistency。短语种保持短文——中英厚文才是送审重量。本线进可索引主内容，不进榜单存档 noindex。

无障碍与 SEO 清单

  • alt 写可见服装实体（贝雷、结、杖、伙伴、卡、翼），禁止「图片1」。
  • 正文重复 Mira Starpetal、magic-girl、魔法少女、封印杖、樱花、original/magic-girl/、video.mp4。
  • 视频带 controls playsinline 与静帧 poster；叙事在首屏 HTML。
  • 不宣称官方 IP 合作；仅个人生成式美术归档。

边界

本稿记录的是本人维护的魔法少女美学学习线（original/magic-girl/）。轮廓借用经典 mahou shōjo 服装语法做一致性训练——不是商业约稿 cos，也不是医疗/金融/法律建议。未成年人观看变身题材时，建议有监护人给予适龄说明。
