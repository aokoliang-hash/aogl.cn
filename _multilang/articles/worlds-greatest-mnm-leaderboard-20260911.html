Worlds Greatest (mnm) — pay-to-rank boards × 3D universe notes

Built by me · App: https://mnm.aogl.cn/ · topics · country boards · bid to rank

2026-09-11 · aogl.cn original

This is a first-party build log on aogl.cn. Screenshots live under original/worlds-greatest/ (archived 2026-09-11). Worlds Greatest（世界之最）is an app I developed myself (footer credit © kolang). App link: https://mnm.aogl.cn/—a public pay-to-display leaderboard where rank equals the bid, topics and countries are first-class filters, and a 3D universe scene (galaxy → solar system → Earth) is the visual stage. This page is not affiliate marketing. It documents my own UI captures—board list, topic chips, country rails, bid modal, profile card, and globe/galaxy CTAs—so search engines and humans can index phrases like “pay to rank leaderboard,” “topic country ranking board,” “3D earth bid to list,” and “mnm.aogl.cn worlds greatest.”

Captures were taken in a browser at https://mnm.aogl.cn/. Pairing structured prose with descriptive alt text beats a bare outbound link: crawlers see entities (topics, countries, bid steps, board totals) in first-paint HTML—not only in WebGL pixels.

Positioning: rank is the bid, nothing else

Most social “leaderboards” pretend merit comes from likes, votes, or opaque algorithms. Worlds Greatest states the contract in the Rules sheet: a public pay-to-rank leaderboard; higher bids rank higher; equal bids: earlier payment wins. Minimum bid starts at ★1; taking #1 requires at least ★1 above the current top. Already listed names can log in and pay only the difference to climb. That is closer to a transparent display-placement market than to a content farm of scraped “richest people” articles.

The product copy is deliberate about legal framing: payments buy display placement only; this is not a lottery or gambling service; payments are final; listings that violate content rules can be removed. Those sentences matter for SEO and for AdSense reviewers: the essay should describe a self-serve ranking UI I built, not invent jackpot language. Gold amounts in the activity feed are that payment, not a cumulative mystery score.

Topics and countries as first-class axes

The top of the board shows topic chips such as #Richest, #Startups, Chinese challenge topics like #比最牛的网站公司 and #比世界最帅, plus + New topic and a “More topics” drawer. Anyone can bid to create a topic when the list is empty—so the taxonomy is user-grown, not a frozen CMS tree. Under topics, a horizontal country rail filters the board: All, China, United States, Japan, South Korea, United Arab Emirates, Sweden, Singapore, Switzerland, Vietnam, Canada, Australia, India, Hong Kong SAR China, and more. The left list is view-only for browsing; your listing country comes from the location sheet (detect + manual country/city edit).

Separating topic from country keeps SEO entities clean. A startup founder in Singapore and a vanity “richest” joke account in Shenzhen do not compete in one undifferentiated scroll. “Today” versus “All-time” further splits attention: Today counts payments in the last 24 hours; All-time keeps the long board. Header stats in captures show on-board counts and total bids—useful social proof without claiming third-party wealth verification.

Bid to rank: the modal chain

Primary CTA copy alternates between Get listed / 我要上榜 and per-row Overtake / Pass. The flow I shipped is intentionally staged:

  1. What to rank — display name; optional description or URL.
  2. Confirm this bid — estimated rank, payment due now, topic, name, country, board total; must agree to Terms.
  3. Choose payment — board stars, currency, method (Stripe/partners in production; Mock pay in dev when methods are disabled).
  4. Secure checkout — popup window with “I’ve paid” return path.

First-time listing does not require login; climbing an existing name does. That tradeoff matches street reality: impulse listing should be one payment deep, while defending a name needs an account so people cannot silently steal someone else’s display string. Share sheets cover WeChat, Weibo, QQ, X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, LinkedIn, download image, and copy link—distribution is part of the product, not an afterthought blog plugin.

Profile cards and latest activity

Tapping a row opens a profile sheet: rank wreath, avatar initial, display name, country flag, star total, optional bio/URL, listed date, and actions Visit homepage, Share, Seize their spot (overtake). Bios are free text—some are jokes (“看我的$$$$$$$$”), some are product plugs, some are blank “Listed YYYY-MM-DD” defaults. The activity ticker explains itself: who passed whom, who paid ★N into which rank. Keeping activity as a feed of payments and passes reinforces the transparent market metaphor and gives the homepage motion without inventing fake “engagement” metrics.

3D stage: galaxy → solar system → Earth

The right half (or full mobile canvas) is not decoration only. Captures show three nested stages:

  • Galaxy / universe — spiral arms, drag to look around; hint text “拖拽环视 · 点击太阳系进入.”
  • Solar system — sun and planets with zoom/reset controls.
  • Earth — rotatable globe with pins; CTAs 「看榜」and 「我要上榜」; hint “拖拽旋转 · 点「看榜 / 我要上榜」 · 返回太阳系.”

Mobile screenshots make the dual CTA pattern obvious: dark 看榜 opens the board; gold 我要上榜 starts listing. The 3D layer exists so the product feels like a place, not a spreadsheet. For SEO, I still describe board mechanics in HTML—WebGL alone is a weak sole signal for crawlers and for AdSense humans who disable heavy scripts.

Why a sibling origin next to aogl.cn

Running the interactive app on mnm.aogl.cn while essays stay on aogl.cn keeps cookies, payment redirects, and WebGL bundles off the editorial static site. WordPet (wordpet.aogl.cn) and EatToday (eattoday.aogl.cn) follow the same pattern: product on a sibling host, production notes on the multilang archive. Archiving timestamped screenshots into original/worlds-greatest/ preserves capture provenance for reviewers. Hero generation still goes through npm run generate-article-covers so Open Graph gets a WebP without stock photography.

Compared with ranking-table archives on this site (Steam charts, Amazon bestsellers—kept noindex), Worlds Greatest belongs in the indexable primary bucket: original product, original screenshots, first-person production notes. It is the same content class as WordPet and EatToday—author-built software documented with UI evidence—even though the product itself is a leaderboard UI.

Workflow notes I refuse to water down

  1. Quote UI labels exactly: 世界之最, 我要上榜, 看榜, All-time, Today, Pass, Overtake, Bid to rank.
  2. Do not claim third-party wealth verification; stars are bids, not audited net worth.
  3. Do not describe the product as gambling; keep “display placement” language from Terms/Privacy/Rules.
  4. Location detection is editable—users must be able to correct country/city before listing.
  5. Dev Mock pay is for staging only; production methods must be enabled by the operator.

Writing while the UI is fresh forces honesty: board counts and #1 names change by the hour; this essay freezes the 2026-09-11 capture set. Chinese body copy serves regional “世界之最 / 上榜” intent; English body copy serves “pay to rank / topic leaderboard / 3D earth board” queries. Short locales (ja/ko/fr/ru/ar) stay brief on purpose—the thick bilingual pair carries AdSense review weight.

Accessibility and SEO checklist

  • Alt text names visible UI entities (topic chips, country flags, CTAs, galaxy/Earth)—never “image 1.”
  • Body text repeats Worlds Greatest, 世界之最, https://mnm.aogl.cn/, bid to rank, All-time, Today, topic, country board, 我要上榜, 看榜.
  • Outbound product links use rel="noopener noreferrer"; full narrative ships in first-paint HTML.
  • Rules concepts (minimum bid, difference payment, non-refundable display placement) are explained in prose so crawlers understand the product without opening modals.

Boundaries

These screens document Worlds Greatest, an app I built (© kolang)—a personal product and UI archive. Board membership, star totals, and topic names change per deployment and capture time. Nothing here claims verified real-world wealth rankings, investment advice, or lottery prizes. Kids should only use payment flows with a guardian.

世界之最 mnm：付费上榜 × 3D 宇宙榜单手记

本人开发 · App：https://mnm.aogl.cn/ · 话题榜 · 国家板 · 出价上榜

2026-09-11 · 本站原创

这是 aogl.cn 上的本人一手产品手记。截图归档在 original/worlds-greatest/（2026-09-11）。「世界之最」是我自己开发的应用（页脚 © kolang）。入口：https://mnm.aogl.cn/——公开的付费展示榜：名次等于出价，话题与国家是一等公民筛选轴，右侧（或移动端全屏）用 3D 宇宙场景（星系 → 太阳系 → 地球）当舞台。本文不是联盟软文，而是对照本人 UI 截图写清楚：榜单列表、话题标签、国家条、出价弹层、个人主页卡、看榜/我要上榜，方便检索「付费上榜」「话题国家榜」「3D 地球上榜」「mnm.aogl.cn 世界之最」等意图。

  • rd.txt — 产品地址：https://mnm.aogl.cn/。

截图均在浏览器打开 https://mnm.aogl.cn/ 时拍摄。结构化正文 + 描述性 alt 比只丢一个外链更利于收录：爬虫能在首屏 HTML 里读到话题、国家、出价步骤、榜单合计，而不是只看见 WebGL 像素。

定位：名次就是出价，没有别的

多数社交「排行榜」把名次归因于点赞、投票或黑盒算法。「世界之最」在规则里写得很直白：公开的付费上榜；出价更高名次更高；同价以先支付者优先。最低出价 ★1；要占 #1，至少比当前第一多 ★1。已上榜名称可登录后只补差价爬升。这更接近透明的展示位市场，而不是爬取「世界富豪」新闻的内容农场。

文案对法律边界同样克制：付钱买的是展示位；不是抽奖、不是赌博；款项一般不退；违规内容可下架不退款。写 SEO / AdSense 手记时要守住这句话：本文描述的是我做的自助上榜界面，不要发明彩票话术。动态里的金色数额是当次支付，不是神秘累计总分。

话题与国家：两条正交轴

榜单上方是话题芯片，例如 #Richest / #最有钱、#Startups，以及中文挑战话题如 #比最牛的网站公司、#比世界最帅，另有 + New topic 与「更多话题」抽屉。列表空时可以出价创建话题——分类是用户长出来的，不是冻死的 CMS 树。话题之下是国家条：全部、中国、美国、日本、韩国、阿联酋、瑞典、新加坡、瑞士、越南、加拿大、澳大利亚、印度、中国香港等。左侧列表只供浏览；真正上榜国家来自定位面板（自动检测 + 手动改国家/城市）。

把话题和国家拆开，实体更干净：新加坡创业者与深圳玩笑号不必挤在同一无差别滚动条里。「今日」与「All-time」再切一刀：今日统计近 24 小时支付；All-time 保留长榜。顶栏的在榜人数、累计出价是社会证明，但不声称第三方财富核验。

出价上榜：弹层链路

主 CTA 在「Get listed / 我要上榜」与行内「Overtake / 抢占」「Pass」之间切换。我落地的流程刻意分段：

  1. What to rank — 展示名；可选简介或 URL。
  2. Confirm this bid — 预估名次、应付金额、话题、名称、国家、榜单合计；需勾选同意条款。
  3. Choose payment — 星数、币种、支付方式（生产环境 Stripe/合作方；开发可 Mock）。
  4. Secure checkout — 弹窗结账，支持「我已支付」回跳。

首次上榜可不登录；给已有名称加价要登录。冲动上榜应「一次支付就结束」，保卫名称则需要账号，以免别人静默抢走展示字符串。分享面板覆盖微信、微博、QQ、X、Facebook、WhatsApp、Telegram、LinkedIn，以及下图与复制链接——分发是产品能力，不是事后插件。

个人主页与最新动态

点开一行进入个人主页：名次花环、头像首字母、展示名、国旗、星数、可选简介/URL、上榜日期，以及访问主页、分享、抢占他的位置。简介是自由文本——有人开玩笑，有人贴产品链，有人只有默认「Listed 日期」。底部动态条解释谁超过了谁、谁付了 ★N 进入哪一名。把动态做成支付与超越的流水，强化「透明市场」隐喻，也给首页动感，而不伪造点赞类指标。

3D 舞台：星系 → 太阳系 → 地球

右侧（或移动端画布）不只是装饰。截图里能看到三层嵌套：

  • 星系 / 宇宙 — 可拖拽环视；提示「拖拽环视 · 点击太阳系进入」。
  • 太阳系 — 太阳与行星，带缩放/复位控件。
  • 地球 — 可旋转地球与钉点；「看榜」「我要上榜」；提示「拖拽旋转 · 点「看榜 / 我要上榜」 · 返回太阳系」。

移动端双 CTA 很清楚：深色看榜打开榜；金色我要上榜开始出价。3D 层让产品像「地方」而不像表格。对 SEO 而言，榜单机制仍必须用 HTML 讲清楚——只靠 WebGL 对爬虫和关闭重脚本的审核员都太弱。

为何放在 mnm.aogl.cn 而不是主站

交互应用跑在 mnm.aogl.cn，长文留在 aogl.cn，可以把 Cookie、支付回跳、WebGL 包与编辑部静态站隔离。WordPet（wordpet.aogl.cn）、EatToday（eattoday.aogl.cn）同一套路：产品在子域，制作手记在多语言归档。时间戳文件名放进 original/worlds-greatest/，方便审核追溯。封面仍走 npm run generate-article-covers，Open Graph 用 WebP，不靠图库。

相对本站那些 Steam / Amazon 类榜单存档（保持 noindex），「世界之最」属于可索引主文：原创产品、原创截图、第一人称过程。与 WordPet、EatToday 同类——作者自建软件 + UI 证据——尽管产品形态本身是榜单界面。

写作时不愿稀释的约束

  1. UI 文案照抄：世界之最、我要上榜、看榜、All-time、Today、Pass、Overtake、Bid to rank。
  2. 不声称第三方财富核验；星数是出价，不是审计净资产。
  3. 不把产品写成赌博；沿用条款里的「展示位」表述。
  4. 定位可改——上榜前必须能纠正国家/城市。
  5. Mock 支付只用于开发；生产支付方式需运营方启用。

趁 UI 还热写完，才能诚实：在榜人数与 #1 名字按小时变；本文冻结的是 2026-09-11 这一组截图。中文服务「世界之最 / 上榜」意图；英文服务 pay-to-rank / topic leaderboard / 3D earth board。短语言包（日/韩/法/俄/阿）故意短——过审重量在中英厚文。

无障碍与 SEO 清单

  • alt 写清可见实体（话题芯片、国旗、CTA、星系/地球），禁止「图片 1」。
  • 正文重复：世界之最、Worlds Greatest、https://mnm.aogl.cn/、出价上榜、All-time、Today、话题、国家板、我要上榜、看榜。
  • 产品外链 rel="noopener noreferrer"；完整叙事在首屏 HTML。
  • 用正文解释最低出价、差价加价、不可退展示位，避免必须点开弹层才懂产品。

边界

这些截图记录的是本人开发的「世界之最」（© kolang）——个人产品与 UI 归档。在榜名单、星数、话题名随部署与拍摄时间变化。本文不声称真实世界财富核验、投资建议或彩票奖金。未成年人参与支付流程需监护人陪同。
