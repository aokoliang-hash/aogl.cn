#1: Blink Plus subscription

The Blink Plus Plan unlocks cloud clip storage and premium features for Blink cameras. Massive review count reflects installed base; holding #1 signals renewal velocity weighs heavily in Best Sellers. Do not confuse with Most Wished For or New Releases — Best Sellers tracks recent sales speed.

Apple stack: #2–4, 6, 8–9

AirTag 2nd Gen (#2) and USB-C EarPods (#3) are high-traffic accessories. Apple Watch Series 11 (#4) and AirPods Pro 3 (#6, ANC + live translation) anchor wearables and flagship TWS. AirPods Max 2 (#8, only 362 ratings but top-10 rank → launch surge) and AirPods 4 (#9, 31K ratings) round out audio. Apple holds six of ten slots — the clearest brand concentration signal on this chart.

Blink hardware: #5 & #7

Blink Video Doorbell (#5) and Blink Outdoor 4 (#7, Sync Module included) pair with the #1 subscription for a hardware + service loop. Add-On SKUs that require a separate Sync Module still rank when review volume and attach rates are strong.

Fire TV: #10

Fire TV Stick 4K Select (#10) with Alexa+ and AI search mirrors deeper-chart Echo Dot and Fire Stick variants (#11–14 in the full scrape). Streaming sticks remain a low-ASP volume lane — compare against Gold Box during Prime events.

Deeper chart (#11–38)

The full capture adds Echo Dot (194K reviews), multiple Fire TV Stick tiers, Beats Solo 4, iPad 11, Kindle lines, Insignia Fire TV 55", and Amazon Smart Plug (571K reviews). Third-party surge protectors, Roku remotes, and budget TWS fill the long tail.

How to use this archive

  • Separate subscription ASINs from hardware when interpreting rank.
  • Low reviews + high rank → new launch; huge reviews + #1 → renewal base.
  • US amazon.com only; pair with our fashion archive for category contrast.

榜首：Blink Plus 云订阅

Blink Plus Plan 为 Blink 摄像头用户的云录像与高级功能订阅。27 万+ 评价反映存量设备基数大；夺魁意味着安防用户续费在 Electronics 榜的订单权重极高。读榜时勿与「Most Wished For」或 New Releases 混淆——Best Sellers 看近期销量速度。

Apple 生态：第 2–4、6、8–9 位

AirTag 第二代（#2，5,436 评价）与 EarPods USB-C（#3，1.4 万+）是低单价高流量配件。Apple Watch Series 11（#4，$279 档）与 AirPods Pro 3（#6，降噪 + 实时翻译）代表可穿戴与 TWS 旗舰线。AirPods Max 2（#8，评价仅 362 条但排名靠前 → 新品冲量）与 AirPods 4（#9，3 万+ 评价）覆盖头戴与中端耳塞。Apple 在 Top 10 占 6 席，是 Electronics 榜最清晰的品牌集中度信号。

Blink 硬件：第 5、7 位

Blink Video Doorbell（#5）与 Blink Outdoor 4（#7，含 Sync Module）与榜首订阅形成「硬件 + 服务」闭环。Doorbell 需另购 Sync Module 的 Add-On 标注影响转化，但 1.2 万 / 2.9 万评价说明安装基盘稳定。

Fire TV：第 10 位

Fire TV Stick 4K Select（#10）带 Alexa+ 与 AI 搜索，与榜内多条 Echo/Fire 产品线（完整榜 #11 Echo Dot、#12–14 多款 Fire Stick）呼应。流媒体棒仍是 Electronics 榜低客单走量品类，适合 Prime Day 前后对照 Deals 榜。

完整榜（#11–38）还透露什么

用户提供的完整抓取中，#11 Echo Dot（19 万+ 评价）、#12–14 Fire TV Stick 全系、#15 Beats Solo 4、#16 iPad 11、#21–22 Kindle Paperwhite / Kindle、#24 Insignia Fire TV 55"、#33 Amazon Smart Plug（57 万+ 评价）等显示：Amazon 第一方 + Apple/Beats 占 Electronics 榜主体，第三方以插线板/遥控/廉价 TWS（#21 surge protector、#26 Roku 遥控、#29 TAGRY 耳塞）填长尾。

如何读 Best Sellers（SEO 与选品）

  • 订阅 vs 硬件 — #1 为服务 SKU；写内容时需区分 ASIN 类型与 LTV。
  • 评价数 vs 排名 — AirPods Max 2 评价少但 #8 → 新品；Blink Plus 评价极多 → 续费盘。
  • 价格 — 表格为抓取时页面显示（示例 TWD，随配送地址变化）。
  • 站点 — 仅 amazon.com 美国站；与 服装榜存档 对照可读消费结构差异。
