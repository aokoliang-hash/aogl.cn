How Apple Trade In works

With Apple Trade In, eligible smartphones can be traded for credit toward a new iPhone at Apple. The official flow:

  1. See what your smartphone is worth — Answer a few questions to get your estimated trade-in value.
  2. Choose your payment method — Trade-in value applies to any payment option. If you choose Buy, credit hits your payment method after inspection. Apple financing applies credit instantly. Carrier financing may apply instant credit or bill credits depending on the carrier. Carrier deals may require switching to a qualifying plan.
  3. Get ready to trade in your device — Keep your old phone until the new one arrives. Apple sends a trade-in kit and email instructions for backup, transfer, and prep — sometimes with the new product, sometimes separately when the order ships.
  4. Ship within 14 days of receiving your kit — After inspection, matching condition completes the trade-in; otherwise Apple emails next steps.

Gift card nuance: When you pay partly or fully with Apple Gift Card or Apple Account balance, trade-in credit applies to those balances first (up to amounts paid with each) before any credit card refund. Excess trade-in value becomes an Apple Gift Card by email.

Recycling when trade-in credit doesn’t apply

If a device doesn’t qualify for trade-in credit, Apple will recycle it for free — prepaid label and packing instructions after purchase. Apple Stores also accept other devices, cables, cases, and monitors for free recycling via Apple Trade In.

Connectivity discounts bake a $30 carrier discount into select advertised iPhone 17 prices when you connect with AT&T, Boost, T-Mobile, or Verizon at checkout — not when you choose “Connect on your own later.”

FAQ highlights from the buy page

  • eSIM only — U.S./Puerto Rico iPhone 16/17 family models have no physical SIM tray; multi-eSIM and dual-number capable.
  • Unlocked by default — apple.com purchases are usually unlocked except AT&T Installment Plan locks.
  • Ready on arrival — Completing carrier activation at checkout means eSIM-ready out of the box; “connect later” activates after delivery.
  • Personal Setup — Free one-on-one online Specialist sessions for data transfer when buying direct.
  • iPhone Upgrade Program — Includes AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss; use the upgrade trade-in path, not standard Trade In, when upgrading mid-program.

Where Pro sits in the 2026 iPhone line

On “Which iPhone is right for you?”, iPhone 17 Pro is “Innovative design for ultimate performance and battery life” — from $1099, 6.3″/6.9″ options, A19 Pro with 6-core GPU, 48MP triple Pro Fusion system with up to 8× optical zoom tiers, USB 3, up to 39 hours video on the larger Pro Max. iPhone Air ($999+) is thinner with a single Fusion camera; iPhone 17 ($799+) uses non-Pro A19; iPhone 17e ($599+) drops ProMotion, Dynamic Island, and Camera Control.

Apple Trade In 四步（官方流程）

  1. 1. 查看旧机估价 — 回答几个问题即可获得预估折抵金额。
  2. 2. 选择付款方式 — 折抵可用于任意付款方式——Apple 分期可即时抵扣；直接购买则在收到并验机后入账；运营商分期则为账单抵扣或即时积分。
  3. 3. 准备交回旧机 — 在新机送达前保留旧机；Apple 寄出以旧换新套件并邮件说明备份与寄回步骤。
  4. 4. 14 天内寄回 — 验机后若状况与估价一致则完成折抵；否则 Apple 邮件告知后续步骤。

Apple Trade In 怎么运作

With Apple Trade In，可在 Apple 官网用符合条件的旧机折抵新机。官方四步概括如下（详见上表）：

  1. 查看旧机价值 — 回答几个问题获得预估折抵价。
  2. 选择付款方式 — 折抵可用于任意付款选项：直接购买则在收到并验机后入账；Apple 分期则即时抵扣；运营商分期则为即时积分或账单抵扣（视运营商而定）。若选 carrier deal，页面会提示是否需要切换至符合资格的套餐。
  3. 准备交回旧机 — 在新机送达前保留旧机；Apple 寄出 trade-in kit 并邮件说明如何备份、迁移数据与打包寄回（可能与新机同箱送达，也可能单独邮寄）。
  4. 14 天内寄回 — 收到 kit 后 14 天内寄出；Apple 验机确认状况与估价一致则完成，否则邮件告知后续步骤。

注意：若部分或全部使用 Apple Gift Card / Apple Account 余额支付，折抵会先冲抵礼品卡或余额（各最多至该方式已付金额），再计入信用卡。若折抵额高于新机价格，差额以 Apple Gift Card 邮件发放。

不符合折抵时：免费回收

若设备不符合 Trade In 折抵条件，Apple 可免费回收：完成购买后邮件发送预付运费标签与打包说明，寄至最近 drop-off 点。亦可到 Apple Store 免费回收其他设备、线缆、保护壳、显示器等，或访问 Apple Trade In 了解其他选项——物品将被再利用或负责任处置。

购买前 FAQ 要点（官方）

  • eSIM — 美国/波多黎各购买的 iPhone 16/17 系列无物理 SIM 托盘，仅 eSIM；可存储多 eSIM、双号码。国际使用需运营商支持 eSIM。
  • 解锁 — 从 apple.com 购买的 iPhone 通常 unlocked；AT&T Installment Plan 例外，合约期内锁定 AT&T 网络。
  • 到货即用 — 若在结账时完成 AT&T/Boost/T-Mobile/Verizon 激活授权，可开箱即用 eSIM；选「稍后连接运营商」则收货后再激活。
  • Personal Setup — 官网购买可免费预约一对一在线 Specialist，协助数据迁移与设置。
  • iPhone Upgrade Program — 含 AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss；勿与标准 Trade In 混用旧机折抵路径。
