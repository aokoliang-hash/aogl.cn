World Cup 2026: Türkiye stuns USMNT 3–2 in stoppage time — USA advances

Kaan Ayhan 90+8' · Trusty 3' · USA wins Group D

2026-06-26 · aogl.cn original

This article archives KCTV5 / Associated Press and FOX Sports coverage of 2026 FIFA World Cup Group D: Türkiye national team vs USMNT in Inglewood, California. Structured data lives in data/world-cup-2026-turkey-usmnt-snapshot.json.

Headline: Turkey’s last-minute goal beats U.S. 3-2 as Americans move on to knockout stage. The United States men’s national team won its opening two group matches over Paraguay and Australia but did not stay perfect — an already eliminated Türkiye side won 3–2 after Kaan Ayhan scored in the final minute of stoppage time.

Context: group already won, rotation and momentum

Per AP/KCTV, the USMNT had already clinched Group D before kickoff; Türkiye was eliminated after two opening losses despite strong underlying stats in those games. On a night where vibes and momentum mattered as much as the table, Mauricio Pochettino made nine changes from the 2–0 win over Australia.

Austin Trusty scored in the third minute — AP notes the second-fastest goal in U.S. World Cup history — pushing the Americans to seven group-stage goals, tying a U.S. scoring record for any World Cup before knockout play began. Türkiye replied through Arda Güler and Orkun Kökçü in the first half; it was the first time the U.S. trailed in the tournament (for about 18 minutes total in the group). Sebastian Berhalter equalized in the 49th minute.

Christian Pulisic, out since the first half of the Paraguay opener with a calf injury, entered in the 58th minute for Tim Weah — his first action in nearly two weeks.

Stoppage-time winner and broadcast clips

With Türkiye’s tournament minutes winding down, Kaan Ayhan scored eight minutes into stoppage time for a 3–2 final. FOX Sports hosts a Türkiye vs United States Highlights reel plus shorter clips (Berhalter equalizer, Güler goal, Ayhan winner). The loss ends a perfect group run but does not change advancement — the story is knockout seeding and form heading into the Round of 32.

Next opponent (report summary)

KCTV/AP report the U.S. advances to the Round of 32 to face Bosnia and Herzegovina in Santa Clara, California, with a 7 p.m. CT kickoff (reported date: Wednesday, June 1, 2026 — confirm against official FIFA schedule).

Kaan Ayhan 90+8' · Trusty 3' · 美国 D 组第一

比赛背景：出线已定，节奏与轮换

据 AP/KCTV，美国男足赛前已锁定 D 组第一；土耳其前两战失利已无法晋级。在这种「结果压力小于走势与士气」的末轮里，毛里西奥·波切蒂诺（Mauricio Pochettino）相对 2–0 胜澳大利亚一战做出九处首发调整。开场第 3 分钟，Austin Trusty 破门为美国取得领先——AP 称这是美国世界杯史上第二快进球；全队小组赛进球数由此达到 7 球，追平美国在淘汰赛开始前的单届世界杯进球纪录。

土耳其在上半场由 Arda Güler 与 Orkun Kökçü 连入两球反超；这是美国在本届世界杯中首次落后，且总共仅落后约 18 分钟。第 49 分钟，Sebastian Berhalter 劲射扳平 2–2。自揭幕战对巴拉圭后因伤缺席的 Christian Pulisic 在第 58 分钟 替补登场，替换 Tim Weah——小腿伤愈后首次回到赛场。

补时绝杀与媒体叙事

在土耳其世界杯之旅临近终场时，Kaan Ayhan 于补时第 8 分钟再下一城，将比分定格为 3–2。FOX Sports 集锦标题强调 Türkiye's Kaan Ayhan scores stoppage-time goal to defeat United States；同页另有 Berhalter 扳平、Arda Güler 进球等片段。对用户提供的导语而言：美国无法在小组赛保持全胜，但出线目标已达成——胜负更多影响淘汰赛对阵与球队势头叙事，而非是否晋级。
