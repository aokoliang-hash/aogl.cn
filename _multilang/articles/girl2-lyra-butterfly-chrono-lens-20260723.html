girl2 · Lyra Chrono-Lens Mistress — butterfly bible and candid scenes

original/girl2 · Chrysalis Gown · six expressions · beach & balcony

2026-07-23 · aogl.cn original

This is a personal character-line archive on aogl.cn. Every file lives under original/girl2/ (dated 2026-07-23). On the design sheet I named her Lyra, Chrono-Lens Mistress—about age 20, lavender hair, violet eyes, with butterflies as the identity motif that survives casual wear, formal gowns, and storyboard panels. Lore-wise she guards a crystalline Chrono Lens that focuses time and stabilizes Temporal Fractures; butterflies appear as a side effect of that work. This page is not a prompt cookbook and not a client delivery memo. It walks through real filenames: a color-coded bible, candid three-views, a six-expression grid, beach and balcony scenes, and a nine-panel “Guardian’s Journey” board—so humans and crawlers can index phrases like “lavender hair character sheet,” “butterfly costume turnaround,” and “AI character consistency candid portrait.”

Why a separate girl2 folder

The site already ships girl1’s beige knit window line and the role-girl white lace card line. Dumping a lavender butterfly ball gown into girl1 would tangle wardrobe meaning; dumping it into role-girl would pollute mini-dress card filenames. girl2 is a batch label—“third face line”—while public titles spell out Lyra / Chrono-Lens / butterfly so search snippets do not collide with knits or lace cards.

For SEO I deliberately repeat entities in prose: Lyra, Chrono-Lens Mistress, Chrysalis Gown, Temporal Fan, lavender hair, butterfly hair clips, candid portrait, Guardian’s Journey. Crawlers meet HTML first; images alone rarely rank long-tail design queries.

What’s in the folder

  • Gemini_Generated_Image_aev3xnaev3xnaev3.png — Lyra bible: front/side/back Chrysalis Gown, part macros, hex palette, six heads.
  • Gemini_Generated_Image_3uqzav…, bewg6l…, du9fbg… — candid FRONT / SIDE / BACK sheets (casual and bust variants).
  • Gemini_Generated_Image_yv7r3e… — six-expression contact grid.
  • t01d973882b3a793849.jpg, jqzxpk… — golden-hour bust anchors (clips + violet iris).
  • 7zbzfl…, wkpb75… — beach knit swimsuit: splash action and over-shoulder still.
  • a7dkkwa…, fgq8p1…, idlsr5… — balcony gown: standing fan, full front, seated butterfly-on-finger.
  • on0tn0… — nine-panel storyboard titled The Guardian’s Journey.

I keep the Gemini filename prefix for batch traceability—same habit as girl1 and sports-lens. I do not paste full vendor prompts here or teach watermark removal.

The bible: butterflies as checkable identity rules

The sheet locks rules you can fail a frame against, not vibe adjectives:

  • Chrysalis Gown — layered lavender tulle, 3D butterfly appliqués on bodice and hem, rear lacing.
  • Temporal Fan — sunset-gold folding fan with wing patterning; held in hand or clipped at the low waist.
  • Pearl Pumps — pearl-white heels so footwear does not mutate into sneakers between scenes.
  • Palette — Lavender #D88FFF, Deep Indigo #480082, Sunset Gold #FFD700, Pearl White #FAEBD7. Beach knit swimsuits are a casual variant that must keep the same hair and clips—not a new face.

The six expression strip (Standard Calm → Worried → Joyful → Focused → Melancholic → Surprised) works like girl1’s contact sheets: a private QA board. If a new render widens the jaw, flattens the bangs, or drops the butterfly clips, it stays out of the keep set.

Candid three-views: casual wear must still turn around

Several “A CANDID PORTRAIT” sheets answer one question: without the ball gown, is she still the same person? Casual kits are usually a cream linen blouse plus clips; some variants add a dusty-rose tulle skirt. Front reads bangs and iris; side reads clip placement along the hairline; back reads the butterfly cluster that pins a half-up. Those three checks matter more than “pretty.”

Bust portraits t01d973882b3a793849.jpg and jqzxpk… play the same role as girl1’s juese1: a ruler for face and hair color. The split warm-sunset / cool-twilight sky is an environmental signature—golden hour plus distant hills—that balcony and beach frames reuse for color-temperature continuity.

I also keep a second candid sheet with landscape backdrops behind each orthographic panel (3uqzav…). It is noisier than the gray-void sheet, but it stress-tests whether silhouette and clip clusters survive busy skies—useful before I trust a marketing crop.

Scene A: beach knit swimsuit (summer casual ladder)

The beach branch swaps Chrysalis tulle for a beige knit/crochet one-piece still pinned with pastel butterflies and pearl strands. Two stills answer different QA questions:

  • 7zbzfl… — waist-deep splash with arms up and water arcing near a heart shape; checks whether motion foam eats facial identity.
  • wkpb75… — over-shoulder glance on sand with footprints and fence posts; checks profile readability under hard backlight.

I keep the swimsuit hand-knit rather than glossy plastic so it belongs with the linen candid blouse in a “tactile casual” family. That builds a clear wardrobe ladder: daily candid → summer beach → ceremonial gown.

Scene B: balcony gown and Temporal Fan

Balcony frames pull the bible gown into a full set: wrought-iron rails with butterfly cutouts, mountain sunset, air filled with purple-gold moths of light. Standing with fan, full front, and seated with a butterfly on the fingertip each fail differently—skirt volume crushing the face, fan motif drifting away from Temporal Fan, bodice butterflies melting into generic floral mush when she sits.

A third balcony still (fgq8p1…) labels itself “UNFILTERED - A CANDID” in the corner. I keep that label in the archive because it marks an exploration pass that still obeys the gown rules—useful when I need to tell “bible-locked” frames from looser mood boards without renaming every PNG.

Storyboard: The Guardian’s Journey

on0tn0… is a nine-panel board titled The Guardian’s Journey. It turns “butterflies = temporal side effect” into beats you can film: parchment butterfly message → golden-hour mountain path → crystal grotto chrysalis → mentor gifting a clip (flashback sketch) → summit butterfly storm → iris awakening macro → winged guardian reveal → cliff temple approach → reprise portrait. I keep sketch cells mixed with fully painted cells on purpose: a storyboard’s job is lens language and pacing, not wallpaper polish in every tile.

Writing the panel titles into this HTML page matters for search: “guardian journey butterfly storyboard,” “chrono lens grotto chrysalis,” and “mountain summit butterfly storm” become indexable strings even if a crawler never OCRs the PNG.

How this line sits beside other aogl.cn characters

  • girl1 — urban window beige knit; grids catch drift.
  • role-girl — white lace cards plus short MP4 tests.
  • Elena — sea-wind game-facing reference.
  • girl2 / Lyra — butterfly identity + bible + candid + storyboard; fantasy ceremony and time motifs.

Running four lines in parallel keeps the home “originals” carousel from looking like one reused face. For AdSense-facing review, this is another thick personal demo: first-screen HTML names files, hex codes, and fail criteria instead of an empty outbound grid.

What I deliberately skipped

There is no interactive HTML demo, no skeletal walk cycle, and no commercial texture pack in this folder. Personality blurbs on the sheet include generative spelling noise; I treat readable names and the palette as anchors and do not treat noisy lore lines as official canon. Assets are personal practice, not licensed studio IP.

I also refuse to collapse beach and gown into one “best of” collage for the hero. Collages look busy in Open Graph previews and hide which file answers which QA question. The cover WebP comes from the bible sheet via npm run generate-article-covers so the carousel shows the turnaround people actually need.

If you copy this folder structure

Recommended order: one palette bible → one face-anchor bust → casual three-view → six-expression grid → two scene families (summer casual + ceremony) → optional nine-panel storyboard. In the article, write what question each file answers. That reads more like an original production note than eight hundred empty adjectives—and it ranks better for concrete queries such as “butterfly character sheet,” “lavender hair turnaround,” and “Chrono-Lens Mistress.”

When the next batch drifts, do not start a girl3 folder until girl2’s keep set is stable. Folder sprawl without a locked face ruler is how identity debt accumulates on disk and in search snippets.

girl2 · Lyra 蝶纹时间透镜：紫发设定表与 candid 场景手记

original/girl2 · Chrysalis Gown · 表情六格 · 海边与阳台

2026-07-23 · 本站原创

这是 aogl.cn 上的一篇个人原创角色线手记，素材全部来自 original/girl2/（2026-07-23 归档）。这条线的名字我在设定表上写成 Lyra, Chrono-Lens Mistress——年龄约 20，紫发紫瞳，以蝴蝶作为贯穿日常服、礼服与剧情板的身份锚点。她被设定为「时间透镜（Chrono Lens）」的守护者：晶体结构用来聚焦时间、稳住 Temporal Fractures，副作用是身边会浮现蝶群。本文不是提示词教程，也不是商单交付说明，而是按真实文件拆解：设定表色板、candid 三视图、表情六格、海边与阳台场景、「Guardian’s Journey」九格叙事，方便检索「紫发角色设定表」「butterfly character design」「AI 角色一致性」等长尾词。

为什么单独开 girl2，而不是塞进 girl1 / role-girl

同站已有 girl1 米色针织窗边线 与 role-girl 白裙卡牌线。若把紫发蝶纹礼服硬塞进 girl1，服装语义会打架；若塞进 role-girl，卡牌短裙与落地 Chrysalis Gown 也会互相污染文件名。我用 girl2 作为批次名：表示「第三条脸线」，对外标题写清 Lyra / Chrono-Lens / butterfly，避免和针织日常、白蕾丝短裙在搜索摘要里撞车。

SEO 上刻意重复实体词：Lyra、Chrono-Lens Mistress、Chrysalis Gown、Temporal Fan、lavender hair、butterfly hair clips、candid portrait。搜索引擎先读 HTML 正文，再读图；只贴图不写设定名，长尾很难落地。

文件夹里有什么

  • Gemini_Generated_Image_aev3xnaev3xnaev3.png — Lyra 设定表：正侧背 Chrysalis Gown、部件特写、色板 hex、六表情。
  • Gemini_Generated_Image_3uqzav… / bewg6l… / du9fbg… — candid FRONT / SIDE / BACK 三视图（便装与半身变体）。
  • Gemini_Generated_Image_yv7r3e… — 表情六格（冷静 / 微笑 / 委屈 / 沉思 / 自信 / 惊讶）。
  • t01d973882b3a793849.jpg / jqzxpk… — 日落半身肖像锚点（蝶夹 + 紫瞳）。
  • 7zbzfl… / wkpb75… — 海边针织泳装：泼水动态与回眸静帧。
  • a7dkkwa… / fgq8p1… / idlsr5… — 阳台礼服：站立、扇子、坐姿蝶停指尖。
  • on0tn0… — 「The Guardian’s Journey」九格叙事板。

文件名保留 Gemini 默认前缀，是为了和 girl1 / sports-lens 一样可追溯批次；我不在文中复述完整 vendor prompt，也不教去水印。

设定表：把蝴蝶写成「可检查的身份规则」

设定表把角色钉死在几条可验收规则上，而不是堆形容词：

  • Chrysalis Gown — 多层薰衣草紫薄纱、胸衣与裙摆布满立体蝶纹，背部有系带结构。
  • Temporal Fan — 日落金色折扇，扇面是蝶翼纹样；可握在手里，也可夹在腰后。
  • Pearl Pumps — 珍珠白高跟鞋，避免鞋型在场景图里突然变成球鞋。
  • 色板 — Lavender #D88FFF、Deep Indigo #480082、Sunset Gold #FFD700、Pearl White #FAEBD7。后续海边米色针织泳装是「日常变体」，仍用同一发色与蝶夹，而不是换一张完全无关的脸。

六表情横条（Standard Calm → Worried → Joyful → Focused → Melancholic → Surprised）的作用和 girl1 的九格一样：给自己看的验收表。任何新图若下颌突然变宽、刘海变齐、蝶夹消失，就不进定稿组。

Candid 三视图：便装也要能正侧背对齐

多张「A CANDID PORTRAIT」三视图回答同一个问题：去掉礼服后，她还是不是同一个人？便装通常是米白亚麻衬衫 + 蝶夹；有的版本加豆沙色多层纱裙。正面看刘海与紫瞳，侧面看蝶夹沿发际线的排布，背面看半扎发上的蝶群发饰——这三点比「漂不漂亮」更重要。

半身肖像 t01d973882b3a793849.jpg 与 jqzxpk… 则扮演 girl1 里 juese1 的角色：脸与发色的尺子。日落左暖右冷的天空，是这条线的环境签名——金小时 + 远山，后面阳台与海边都复用同一色温逻辑。

场景 A：海边针织泳装（日常夏日变体）

海边线把 Chrysalis 礼服换成米色针织 / 钩织连体泳装，仍钉着彩色蝶饰与珍珠链。两张关键帧分工不同：

  • 7zbzfl… — 腰部入水、双手扬起、水花近似心形，测动态与水花是否淹没脸部身份。
  • wkpb75… — 沙滩回眸、脚印与栅栏，测侧脸与发丝在强逆光下是否仍可读。

我刻意让泳装保持「手工针织纹理」，而不是光滑塑料感比基尼——这样和亚麻 candid 衬衫同属「触感日常」，与薄纱礼服形成清晰的服装阶梯：日常 → 夏日 → 典礼。

场景 B：阳台礼服与 Temporal Fan

阳台线把设定表里的 Chrysalis Gown 拉进完整环境：铸铁栏杆带蝶形镂空、远山日落、空气中满是紫金蝶群。站立持扇、正面全身、坐姿指尖停蝶，三张回答不同验收问题：裙摆体积是否压过人脸、扇子纹样是否仍像 Temporal Fan、坐姿时胸衣蝶纹会不会糊成一团花。

叙事板：The Guardian’s Journey

on0tn0… 是九格故事板，标题写着 The Guardian’s Journey。它把「蝴蝶 = 时间副作用」写成可见情节：收到羊皮纸蝶纹讯息 → 金小时山路启程 → 水晶岩洞发现茧源 → 导师赠蝶发饰闪回 → 云海峰顶蝶暴考验 → 虹膜觉醒特写 → 蝶翼觉醒 → 崖边神殿就任 → 新标准肖像收束。我保留素描格与成色格混排，是为了提醒自己：叙事板的价值在镜头语言与节拍，不在每一格都要发成壁纸。

和本站其它角色线怎么分工

  • girl1 — 城市窗边米色针织，九格查漂移。
  • role-girl — 白蕾丝短裙卡牌 + MP4。
  • Elena — 海风游戏向参考。
  • girl2 / Lyra — 蝶纹身份 + 设定表 + candid + 叙事板，偏奇幻典礼与时间母题。

四条线并行，首页「本站原创」才会出现不同封面，避免访客以为全站只有一张脸。对 AdSense 审核面而言，这也是持续的个人 Demo：首屏 HTML 写清文件名、色板与验收规则，而不是空壳外链墙。

我刻意没做的事

文件夹里没有可交互 HTML Demo、没有骨骼 walk 循环、没有商用贴图导出。设定表上的性格短句有生成噪声（拼写不规则），我只把可读的设定名与色板当锚点，不把噪声文案当官方 lore 对外背书。素材是个人练习归档，不是出版社或游戏工作室授权资产。

若你要抄这套文件夹结构

推荐顺序：一张带色板的设定表 → 一张脸锚点肖像 → 便装三视图 → 表情六格 → 两套场景（日常夏日 + 典礼） → 可选九格叙事板。每张图在文章里写清「回答什么问题」，比堆八百个形容词更像原创手记——也更容易被检索到「butterfly character sheet」「lavender hair turnaround」「Chrono-Lens Mistress」这类具体查询。
