Instagram’s Popular page aggregates what Meta surfaces as heavily discussed content for logged-out or light web browsing: hashtag rows with public post counts plus a Reels thumbnail grid labeled by keyword phrases. Unlike Google Trends search spikes, Popular skews toward visual social, sports, entertainment, and lifestyle moments.

Trending snapshot

What the topics signal

FIFA World Cup 2026 · Mundial / Copamundial / Vamos

Mundial (~20.25M posts), Copamundial, Vamos, Ecuador, Seleccionmexicana, Vivamexico, and Estadio Azteca dominate — aligned with the North America 2026 tournament window. Cross-read our World Cup match archive and Portal Hub Google Trends RSS pulls.

Wimbledon

Wimbledon (~11.63M posts) marks grass-court season peak — outfit posts, score cards, and celebrity court-side photos are classic IG formats.

US Independence Day & citizenship discourse

Independenceday, Birth Right Citizenship, Citizenship, Immigration, and Constitution tags cluster around early-July civic debate mixed with holiday lifestyle content.

Basketball · LeBron / Clippers

Lebron James (~32.68M posts) sits alongside Los Angeles Clippers and WNBA tags; Reels themes include lamelo ball trade, nba draft awkward moments 2024, and bronny.

Summer heat & travel

Extreme Heat, Summerheat, Staycool, Airconditioning, and Cruise Life reflect seasonal lifestyle posting in the Northern Hemisphere.

Reels grid themes

Thumbnail labels read like search phrases: combat (justin gaethje arman tsarukyan feud), soccer (lamine yamal goal, ronaldo goal), gaming (fortnite, deltarune), K-pop (stray kids), and TV (one piece, loveisland). They describe recommendation clusters, not single creator accounts.
