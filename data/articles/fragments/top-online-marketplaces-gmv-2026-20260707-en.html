As global eCommerce expands, online marketplaces shape the retail landscape. The top 100 marketplaces are projected to generate $3.8 trillion in gross merchandise value (GMV) at roughly 10% annual growth. For sellers and brands, understanding platform scale and regional dynamics is essential for channel strategy.

Top five online marketplaces by GMV (data table)

1. Taobao ($723.8B GMV)

Taobao (Alibaba Group) leads globally by GMV, blending C2C and B2C and driving China’s eCommerce boom.

Key strengths

  • Diverse range — vast SKU pool from daily goods to niche categories.
  • Integrated experience — Alipay, live commerce, and social shopping in the purchase flow.

Insight: Taobao’s scale reflects China’s population and digital infrastructure; for global brands, the Alibaba ecosystem remains a primary entry point.

2. Tmall ($682.7B GMV)

Tmall, also under Alibaba, ranks second with a B2C focus on official brand flagship stores.

Key strengths

  • Premium positioning — attracts quality brands and affluent shoppers.
  • Cross-border — Tmall Global supports import and outbound brand expansion.

Insight: Tmall’s “authentic + branded” trust profile makes it the main battlefield for premium and global labels in China.

3. Amazon ($435.7B GMV)

Amazon ranks third on third-party GMV at $435.7B, continuing to set benchmarks in logistics, Prime loyalty, and tech investment.

Key strengths

  • Prime membership — fast shipping and exclusive deals drive retention.
  • Global footprint — multi-market operations plus FBA logistics.

Insight: Amazon’s influence extends beyond retail into AI and cloud (AWS). See our Electronics Best Sellers and Prime Day 2026 archives.

4. JD.com ($249.9B GMV)

China’s JD.com places fourth at $249.9B GMV, known for self-operated retail and integrated logistics.

Key strengths

  • Speed and reliability — same-day and next-day delivery in key cities.
  • Technology — warehouse automation, drones, and AI routing.

Insight: JD competes strongly in 3C, appliances, and authenticity — a dual-giant landscape vs Alibaba.

5. Shopee ($77.2B GMV)

Southeast Asia’s Shopee (Sea Limited) rounds out the top five at $77.2B GMV with a mobile-first, localized playbook.

Key strengths

  • Gamified shopping — interactive promos, flash sales, and live commerce.
  • Localization — country teams across Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines, Thailand, and more.

Insight: Shopee signals Southeast Asia’s growth runway — smaller than China’s trio but attractive for cross-border sellers.

Reading GMV rankings

  • Methodology — SaleHoo’s third-party seller GMV may differ from ECDB or company filings that include 1P retail.
  • Regions — three of five are China-based; Amazon leads outside Asia; Shopee represents SEA.
