YouTube and FIFA announced the first-ever YouTube FIFA Creator Cup, streaming globally on YouTube only from New York City on July 12, 2026, featuring creators, elite athletes, and celebrities (captains and full rosters TBA). They also unveiled 24 global YouTube creators for on-the-ground World Cup 2026 coverage—350+ million combined subscribers—spanning tactical breakdowns, pitch challenges, local food culture, and behind-the-scenes moments alongside official media partners’ YouTube coverage.

What the Creator Cup is

An exhibition under YouTube’s Preferred Platform partnership with FIFA—not a World Cup match, but a flagship digital event during the tournament window. Search terms: YouTube FIFA Creator Cup 2026, World Cup creator exhibition match.

How to read the roster

Creators span the US, Mexico, Brazil, the UK, Belgium, Canada, Japan, South Korea, Kazakhstan, Indonesia, and the UAE. Sports-challenge channels (Jesser, Deestroying, Zhong), football-storytellers (The Sidemen, Neagle, Viniblogger, Kwakyoongy), lifestyle/travel hosts (Ashley Alexander, Kelly Wakasa, Kika Kim), and comedy/Shorts creators (Anwar Jibawi, Horchata Soto, Sonrixs, Jenny Hoyos) cover different fan entry points.
