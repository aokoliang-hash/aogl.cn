Trending Trailers ranks movie and series trailer videos by US attention on YouTube, separate from the main Trending tab or Music Charts. The table mixes studio uploads with breakdown and reaction channels — major IP launches often show one official row at #1 and several creator videos below. Use the “Type” column to tell them apart.

Chart data (top 10)

#1: Spider-Man: Brand New Day

On June 17, 2026, Sony Pictures Entertainment posted the 4K trailer, which quickly hit #1 on Trending Trailers. Marvel Studios branding, Tom Holland as Peter Parker, and the dual-identity glass-tower shot drove shareable thumbnails across social and search.

Ranks #3 and #6 are breakdown videos from New Rockstars and ScreenCrush (Easter eggs, X-Men teases). That pattern — official first, creators next — is normal for MCU trailer weeks.

#2–5: Animation and anime

Shrek 5 (Universal, June 16) holds #2. Chainsaw Man – Assassins Arc Official Trailer 2 (MAPPA, June 19) is #4. Hexed (Disney Animation teaser, #5) and Gatto (Pixar teaser at #10) show two Disney animation pipelines in the same chart week.

#7–10: Streaming and theatrical

Outer Banks S5 (Netflix teaser, #7) shows series launches competing in a “movie trailers” chart. Heart of the Beast (Paramount, #8) and Whalefall (20th Century, #9, Oct 16 release) are traditional theatrical pushes.
