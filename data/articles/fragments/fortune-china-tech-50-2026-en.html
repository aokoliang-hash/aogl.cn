Top 5 highlights

1 · Huawei Investment & Holding (HUAWEI)

Huawei retains #1. Public reporting cites ~CNY 192.3 billion R&D in 2025 (21.8% of revenue) and >CNY 1.38 trillion cumulative R&D over the past decade. From Kirin and Ascend AI silicon to HarmonyOS and openEuler, Huawei anchors the list’s “root technology + long-term R&D” narrative.

2 · Beijing ByteDance Technology (ByteDance)

ByteDance ranks second as one of the most globally distributed AI application companies on the list. TikTok and Doubao underpin worldwide reach; reports highlight heavy AI infrastructure spend. Beijing leads nationally with 14 entries — ByteDance represents internet and large-model strength in that cluster.

3 · Contemporary Amperex Technology (CATL)

CATL is third, reflecting China’s battery and new-energy supply chain moat in EVs and storage. The broader list also spans Jinko Solar, Trina Solar, GCL Tech, Easpring, and BYD — CATL is the cell-scale anchor.

4 · Tencent Holdings (Tencent)

Tencent ranks fourth. Hunyuan models plus WeChat-scale super-app infrastructure keep Tencent platform-relevant in the AI era. Among the internet giants, the Tech 50 framing stresses R&D and model capability, not application-layer monetization alone.

5 · DeepSeek

DeepSeek debuts in the top five — the list’s standout foundation-model story. Industry coverage focuses on an independent AGI architecture path, large financing rounds, and compute-ecosystem build-out. DeepSeek’s placement signals base models are now infrastructure peers alongside Huawei and ByteDance, not merely viral apps.

Sectors and geography

Fifty companies span AI, semiconductors, new energy, biopharma, embodied intelligence, commercial space, and quantum computing. Beyond Beijing’s 14 entries, Shenzhen (BYD, Unitree, CREALITY, Dahua), Shanghai (MetaX, Biren, STARTORUS FUSION), and Jiangsu (VGT, SPRINT, JSHP) each contribute representative names.

AI & LLMs: DeepSeek, Moonshot AI (Kimi), Tsingmicro, Kunlunxin, Biren. Robotics & embodied AI: Unitree, XYZ Robotics, NEOLIX, ESTUN. Semiconductors & advanced manufacturing: VGT, MetaX, SPRINT, Goertek, BOE. New energy & materials: BYD, Jinko Solar, Trinasolar, Easpring. Frontier science: QBoson (quantum), STARTORUS FUSION (fusion energy).
