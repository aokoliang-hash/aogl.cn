This article archives the AICPB Global AI Website Rankings — Issue 37 (June 2026 edition): the full Top 100 chart ranked by monthly website visits. The official page was last updated 2026-07-08. Structured data: data/aicpb-global-ai-website-rankings-202606-snapshot.json.

AICPB positions itself as a global AI rankings standard-setter, updating monthly and cited by outlets such as Reuters and The Information. Unlike our April 2026 excerpt (Website/App Top 10 each), this page focuses on the complete Website top 100 so readers can look up any product’s rank and month-over-month (MoM) change.

Global · Website Top 100

Top 3: general-purpose AI portals still dominate

1 · ChatGPT — 5.56B visits (-3.29%)

ChatGPT leads with roughly 5.56 billion monthly visits, MoM -3.29%. Even with a slight decline, it remains the traffic anchor for conversational AI — still more than 1.8B visits ahead of #2.

2 · New Bing — 3.68B (+0.73%)

New Bing records 3.68B visits, MoM +0.73%, the only positive mover in the top three. Microsoft’s Copilot-in-search narrative keeps Bing’s web footprint resilient in H1 2026.

3 · Gemini — 2.95B (-1.51%)

Google Gemini holds ~2.95B visits, MoM -1.51%. Gemini remains the third-largest AI web entry point as Google embeds generative features across Search, Workspace, and AI Studio.

Ranks 4–10: Claude, DeepSeek, and Chinese search

Claude | Anthropic (#4, 965.59M, -0.61%) stays near the 1B visit tier after April’s +34% surge cooled. DeepSeek (#5, 483.80M, -10.59%) sees a pullback in overseas web traffic. GitHub Copilot (#6) and Shop (#7) represent stable dev-tool and commerce-AI baselines.

notebooklm, Grok, and Character AI fill #8–10. Chinese products Nami AI Search (#11) and Doubao (#12) already sit in the global top fifteen — evidence that multi-agent search and chat assistants from China are scaling on the open web.

Extreme MoM swings: Notion AI and detectors

The steepest drop is Notion AI (#50, 24.47M, -84.43% MoM). Such a move often reflects measurement or URL consolidation rather than pure churn, but it signals how AI features folded into a primary workspace can shift traffic attribution.

AI detection and academic integrity tools broadly softened: GPTZero (-28.56%), Q-Chat (Quizlet) (-28.44%), Turnitin (-26.24%), and GenSpark (-25.07%) all fell double digits — possibly seasonal (end of school term) or competition from native “AI labels” inside LLM products.

Fastest growers: Zhipu, Dreamina, roleplay

Z.ai | Zhipu (#86, +24.56%) tops the gainers list among the full 100. Dreamina (+23.51%), Pollo AI (+18.96%), and JanitorAI (+14.94%) follow — image/video AIGC and character chat remain acquisition-heavy niches. Baidu AI Search (+13.76%) and polybuzz (+13.20%) show Chinese search/social AI recovering on the web.

Among dev tools, Cursor (#53, +7.22%) continues climbing in the AI-IDE wave; opencode (+11.10%) and OpenRouter (+2.81%) illustrate long-tail growth in coding agents and model routing.

Ranks 11–50: China matrix and SaaS AI

Chinese-linked entries in the top 50 include Nami AI Search, Doubao, Nami AI, Baidu AI Search, Qianwen, Qwen.ai, Kimi, and Baidu Wenku — spanning search, chat, documents, and creation. On the SaaS side, Salesforce AI (+10.03%) and HubSpot (+9.31%) gain as CRM/marketing platforms embed agents, while Perplexity AI (-16.15%) retrenches as AI search competition intensifies.

Ranks 51–100: creative stack and infrastructure

The back half lists image/video (Midjourney, Leonardo.Ai, HeyGen, Jimeng AI), audio (Suno, ElevenLabs, Adobe Podcast), 3D (Meshy), and infra (Supabase, Vercel, Hugging Face, Ollama). Unity (#100, 8.44M) edges in — real-time 3D tooling still registers measurable AI-adjacent web demand.
